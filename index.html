06 / THE METHOD

Learn the journey.
Learn to arrive.

Taomate-F1 trains a causal audiovisual generator to connect two prescribed images. Its training connects local generation decisions to the quality of the complete transition.

FIRST FRAME
Target image · positioned at the terminal time↓    ↓    ↓
AVblock 1→AVblock 2→AVblock …

Clean audiovisual history moves forward →

TARGET FRAME
01

Temporal conditioning & trajectory supervision

The target image is encoded at its global terminal time. Paired teacher trajectories supervise intermediate spatial structure and temporal change, alongside endpoint reconstruction and distribution matching.

02

Full-chain denoising gradients

Output supervision reaches every retained denoising step within a block. Early computations learn how they affect the content that is ultimately emitted.

03

Cross-Block GRPO

Later policy losses propagate through selected history paths into earlier content generation. Quality and terminal-image rewards express preferences over complete audiovisual rollouts.

Guide the next chapter, without erasing the past.

The continuation demonstration reuses the actual generated boundary frame and retained audiovisual context, then introduces another target image. This turns a single first-to-last transition into a repeatable storytelling process.
